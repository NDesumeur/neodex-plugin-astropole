# Astropolis

A pixel-art incremental game, rebuilt from scratch in version 2. You build a space city whose buildings make credits every second, even when NeoDex is closed. The game has four layers, and each one changes the rules: the city, colonies, singularities, then the galaxy.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## The city

- **Click the city**: each click brings credits.
- **Buy buildings** in the Buildings tab, with the ×1, ×10, ×100 or MAX buttons. They go from the habitat module to the void architect, then to megastructures in orbit, without end.
- Every **25 copies** of a building, its production **doubles**.
- Each kind of building has **its own district**. Small ones stand in front and towers behind. A district fills up and grows as you buy more.
- **Double-click a district** to see it up close: its inhabitants walk, chat and dance. The ← City button (or Esc) goes back to the overview.
- **Day and night** pass every 4 minutes. There are auroras, a monorail, flying cars, rockets and clouds.

## Events

- **A ship arriving** or **a party** in a district: a bubble shows above it. Click it to zoom in, then click the ship (2 minutes of production) or the party (production ×3 for 2 minutes).
- **Meteor shower**: click the meteors.
- **The merchant** comes by in a golden ship: pick one bonus out of three.
- **Yellow cargo ships** bring 30 seconds of production.

## Colonies

- **Research**: each building ×3 at 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 and 400 copies, links between buildings, stronger clicks.
- **Doctrine**: in each colony, you pick a single path out of three, with four tiers.
  - **Industry**: a city that runs itself (lower prices, ×2.5 milestones, automatic buying).
  - **Science**: cheaper research, Memories ×2, achievements worth double.
  - **Trade**: clicks ×10, more frequent events, a more generous merchant.
- **New colony** (Colony tab): after 1 trillion credits made, you start over on the next planet with **Memories**. A Memory kept adds 10 % production. Spent, it buys a perk.
- Each planet has its strengths: Cryos, Pyra, Sylve, Aurora and Nebula.

## Challenges

They open after 5 colonies or at the first singularity. A challenge restarts the colony with a constraint, and winning it gives a bonus for good. There are eight:

- Click blackout
- Inflation
- No research
- Isolation
- Eclipse
- Dead calm
- Specialist
- Amnesia

## Singularities

Once **1e50 credits** are made, the city can **collapse into singularities**. Everything starts from zero, Memories included. What you get in return changes the game:

- **Dark matter** appears and grows over time. It multiplies all production by (1 + dark matter)².
- **Laws of physics** are bought with singularities:
  - Automatons buy buildings by themselves;
  - Living archives buy research;
  - Polymath gives two doctrines at once;
  - with Tight milestones, a building doubles every 10 copies;
  - with the Wormhole, time away counts double.
- **Distortions** are bought with dark matter: time dilation, price compression, megastructure gravity, Memory echo.

A game started before version 2 counts everything it already made toward the first singularity.

## The galaxy

With **30 singularities** earned, the **exodus** gives **Stars**. The galaxy map has 22 systems linked by routes. You conquer the systems next to yours, and each one gives a bonus for good:

- production ×1,000;
- dark matter ×3;
- singularities ×1.5;
- Memories ×10;
- cheaper buildings;
- more frequent events;
- a richer start.

## Coming back, and the numbers

- When you come back, an **away report** shows what was made and the events you missed. It lets you choose:
  - **cash in**;
  - **reinvest** right away;
  - throw a **welcome-back party** (a quarter of the gains for production ×3 during 15 minutes);
  - **call the merchant back**.
- The **Statistics** tab shows production over time, over 2 days or 30 days, with colonies, challenges, singularities and exoduses.
- There you also pick the **notation for big numbers**: names (millions, billions), scientific, engineering, letters or logarithm.
- There are **101 achievements**. Each one adds 3 % production for good.

The **Full screen** button is at the top of the page. The game stays on this device. A version 1 game carries on as it was.
