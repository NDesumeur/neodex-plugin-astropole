# Astropole

An incremental management game: build a space colony whose buildings produce credits every second, even while NeoDex is closed. Reinvest your credits in new buildings, unlock ever bigger constructions, then found a new colony to produce even faster.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## How to play

- **Buy buildings** from the list: from the habitat module to the hydroponic greenhouse, the solar field, the asteroid mine… up to the black hole engine and the void architect. Each one produces credits continuously.
- The **×1**, **×10**, **×100** and **MAX** buttons choose how many to buy at once. Each copy costs 15% more than the previous one.
- **Click the city** to earn one second of production, and a **golden cargo ship** when it flies by to earn thirty.

## Progression

- Every **25 copies** of the same building **double** its production.
- Each new type of building you buy unlocks another one. After the first sixteen come the **megastructures**, endlessly.
- Numbers have no limit: production can climb far beyond billions.

## Time passing

The colony keeps producing while NeoDex is closed. When you open it, all the time since the last save is counted, on any of your PCs, and the game shows what you earned while you were away.

## New colony

Once the colony has produced **1 trillion credits** (10¹²), the **New colony** button lets you start over and earn **Memories**. Each Memory adds **10% production forever**, in every later colony. The game asks you to type "oui" to confirm, since credits and buildings go back to zero.

## Data and sync

Your game, resources and unlocks are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. No other permission, no Internet connection.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Astropole → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
