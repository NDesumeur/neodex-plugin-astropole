# Astropole

A pixel art idle game: build a space city whose buildings make credits every second, even when NeoDex is closed. Each kind of building has its own district, the city fills up, and day and night pass on six different planets.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## How to play

- **Click the city**: each click brings at least 1 credit, plus one second of production. The first buildings are cheap for a quick start, and a tip always tells you what to do next.
- **Buy buildings**: from the habitat module to the greenhouse, the solar field, the mine… up to the black hole engine and the void architect. The **×1**, **×10**, **×100** and **MAX** buttons choose how many to buy at once.
- Every **25 copies** of the same building, its production **doubles**. After the first sixteen come the **megastructures**, stations in orbit, without end.

## A living city

- Each kind of building has **its own district**: small buildings in front, towers behind, giants at the back. The more you buy, the more the district fills up and its buildings grow. The next building to unlock already has its building site.
- **Day and night** pass every 4 minutes: sunrise and sunset, stars and auroras, windows and street lamps lighting up.
- Passers-by, hover cars, a monorail, rockets taking off from the spaceport, clouds and ships in the sky.

## Research and achievements

- The **Research** tab offers upgrades to buy: each building ×3 at 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 and 400 copies, **links** (each building boosts the next one by 1 %) and clicks twice as strong.
- The **Achievements** tab counts 73 achievements. Each adds **3 % production for good**.

## Events

- **Meteor shower**: click the meteors to earn credits.
- **The merchant** comes by in a golden ship: click it and pick one bonus out of three (production ×7, clicks ×20, buildings half price…).
- The **yellow cargo ships** bring 30 seconds of production when you click them.

## Time passing

The colony produces even when NeoDex is closed. On opening, all the time since the last save is counted, on any of your PCs, and the game shows what you earned while away.

## New colony

Once **1 trillion credits** are produced, **New colony** starts everything over on the **next planet** and gives you **Memories**. Each planet has its strengths: Cryos (refineries ×10), Pyra (mines ×10, clicks ×3), Sylva (greenhouses ×10, more frequent events), Aurora (spaceports ×5, more frequent cargo ships), Nebula (laboratories ×10, cheaper research).

A Memory kept adds 10 % production; in the **Memories** tab you can also spend it on **perks** for good: Legacy (production ×2), Golden hands (clicks ×5), Architects (cheaper buildings), Foundations (more credits at the start), Lucky star (more frequent events).

The **Fullscreen** button is at the top of the page.

## Data and sync

Your game, resources and unlocks are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. No other permission, no Internet connection.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Astropole → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
